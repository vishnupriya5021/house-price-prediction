Our House Price Prediction web application in DevOps is designed to provide accurate, real-time property valuations while ensuring seamless development and deployment. The system automates the entire pipeline—from model training and testing to deployment and monitoring—allowing updates to be integrated without delays. By combining machine learning with DevOps practices, the application delivers reliable predictions, improves collaboration between teams, and ensures continuous delivery. The purpose of this application is to offer users quick, data-driven insights into house prices while maintaining high quality and scalability through DevOps.
